# Privacy

SVARĀ is an offline music player. In short: your music, your data, your device.

- **No network.** The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission, so it cannot connect to anything. There is no telemetry, analytics, advertising, account, cloud sync, online lyrics or online artwork.
- **Local files only.** SVARĀ reads the audio files already on your device, through the media library or the folders you choose. It never changes or deletes your audio files.
- **What it stores, on the device only:** the library index, playlists, favourites, listening history (which you can pause or erase in Settings › Backup and data › Privacy), settings, saved looks, custom covers you add (copied into app-private storage), recap summaries computed from your history, and a short crash log.
- **Recaps** are worked out on the device from your listening history. Erasing history also erases recaps.
- **Sharing is your choice.** A recap image is created only when you tap Share or Save, and goes only where you send it through the system share sheet.
- **Backup is your choice.** Export writes one file to a location you pick. It holds settings, playlists, favourites, history and cover assignments, but never audio or cover images. Restore reads only a file you pick.
- **Crash log** summaries stay in app-private storage. You can read, export or clear them in Settings › Backup and data › Crash log. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.
